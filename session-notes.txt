Proof entry points:
- AbsoluteConvergence: geometric bounds iff absolute summability at a positive
  polyradius. PowerSeriesBounds and ConvergenceAlgebra: algebra and derivatives.
- Majorants and WordMajorant: the quantitative iterated-vector-field estimate,
  using the nonnegative series F_I = C^|I| and partial_i F <= C F^2.
- Factorials and FactorialEstimate: multi-index comparison and common bounds.
- NecessaryConvergence: finitely many Lie polynomials induce convergent fields,
  so their ordered products satisfy condition (C).
- ResidualConvergence: prepend letters to the tested family, iterate, and use
  polynomial linearity; centering preserves (C).
- WeightedMass, Composition, Reciprocal: weighted coefficient sums control
  substitution and geometric series, giving composition and scalar inversion.
- InverseFrame, TaylorCoefficients, AnalyticInverse: conjugated coordinate
  derivations form the inverse Jacobian. Adjugates and reciprocal convergence
  make its coefficients analytic. The word estimate gives geometric Taylor
  bounds for inverse coordinates. This proves the analytic inverse theorem
  directly, using the earlier factorial estimate rather than an external IFT.
- PBWConvergence: cancel I! in the PBW expansion coefficient formula.
- CoordinateEquivalence and AnalyticCoordinates: transfer convergent expansions
  from PBW coordinates to arbitrary normalized coordinates.
- Existence: normalized observation residuals define the analytic realization.
- Realization: characterization and least dimension from existence, necessity,
  and the formal rank lower bound. Uniqueness: the formal equivalence and its
  inverse converge by the analytic coordinate theorem.
